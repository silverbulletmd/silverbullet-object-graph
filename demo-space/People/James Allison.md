James Allison is Technical Director of [[Teams/Mercedes]], returning to the role in 2023 after a brief stint as Chief Technical Officer. His career includes design leadership at the Enstone factory and at [[Teams/Ferrari]].

He works closely with [[People/Toto Wolff]] on Mercedes's technical strategy.

Pierre Waché has been Technical Director of [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]] since 2018, working under [[People/Adrian Newey]] before taking lead technical responsibility after Newey's 2024 departure for [[Teams/Aston Martin]]. He led the RB19 and RB20 design effort during [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]]'s dominant seasons.

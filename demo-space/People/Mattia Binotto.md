Mattia Binotto spent 28 years at [[Teams/Ferrari]], rising from engine engineer to Team Principal from 2019 to 2022. After a year out, he joined [[Teams/Audi]] in mid-2024 as COO/CTO, leading the Hinwil project as it transitions from Sauber to the full Audi works programme for 2026.

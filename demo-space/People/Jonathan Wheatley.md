Jonathan Wheatley spent nearly two decades as Sporting Director at [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]], coordinating multiple championship campaigns including four titles with [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]]. In 2025 he moved to Hinwil as Team Principal of the incoming [[Teams/Audi]] works team, working alongside [[People/Mattia Binotto]].

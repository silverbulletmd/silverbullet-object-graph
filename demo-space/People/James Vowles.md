James Vowles became Team Principal of [[Teams/Williams]] in 2023, brought in from a senior strategy role at [[Teams/Mercedes]] under [[People/Toto Wolff]]. He is leading a long-term rebuild centred on [[Drivers/Alex Albon]] and [[Drivers/Carlos Sainz]].

Andrea Stella has been Team Principal at [[Teams/McLaren]] since 2023, overseeing the team's resurgence to the 2024 constructors' title with [[Drivers/Lando Norris]] and [[Drivers/Oscar Piastri]]. He previously worked as race engineer to [[Drivers/Fernando Alonso]] at [[Teams/Ferrari]].

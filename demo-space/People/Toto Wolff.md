Toto Wolff has been Team Principal and CEO of [[Teams/Mercedes]] since 2013, joining from a senior role at [[Teams/Williams]]. He holds a one-third equity stake in the team.

Under his leadership Mercedes won eight consecutive constructors' titles from 2014 to 2021 with [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]].

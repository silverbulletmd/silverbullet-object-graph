Adrian Newey is the most successful Formula 1 designer of the modern era. His championship-winning cars at [[Teams/Williams]] (1992–1997), [[Teams/McLaren]] (1998), and [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]] (2010–2013, 2021–2024) span four decades.

He left Red Bull in 2024 — a long-rumoured departure linked to the [[People/Christian Horner]] situation — and joined [[Teams/Aston Martin]] in a combined Managing Technical Partner and Team Principal role. He has worked with champions including [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]].

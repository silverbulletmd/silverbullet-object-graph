The 2024 season saw [[Teams/McLaren]] take their first constructors' title since 1998 with [[Drivers/Lando Norris]] and [[Drivers/Oscar Piastri]], while [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]] secured a fourth consecutive drivers' title. [[Drivers/Sergio Pérez]] was dropped at year-end; [[Drivers/Logan Sargeant]] was replaced mid-season by [[Drivers/Franco Colapinto]]; [[Drivers/Daniel Ricciardo]] was replaced by [[Drivers/Liam Lawson]] at the Faenza team. Ten teams contested the championship.

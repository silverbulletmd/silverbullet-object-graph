The Autodromo Nazionale Monza, "the Temple of Speed", has hosted the Italian Grand Prix nearly every year since 1950. It is the spiritual home of [[Teams/Ferrari]] and the tifosi.

Notable recent winners include [[Drivers/Pierre Gasly]]'s shock 2020 victory and multiple [[Drivers/Charles Leclerc]] poles.

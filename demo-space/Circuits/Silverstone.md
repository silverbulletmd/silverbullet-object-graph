Silverstone hosted the first ever Formula 1 World Championship round on 13 May 1950. Home to the British Grand Prix and to many of the constructors themselves — [[Teams/Aston Martin]], [[Teams/Mercedes]] (Brackley), [[Teams/Williams]] (Grove), and [[Teams/Racing Bulls]] (UK technical office) all sit within easy reach.

Recent winners include [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] (a record nine times) and [[Drivers/Lando Norris]].

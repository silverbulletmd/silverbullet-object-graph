Bottas partnered [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] at [[Teams/Mercedes]] from 2017 to 2021, winning ten races. After a stint at Sauber he was a Mercedes reserve in 2025, then signed with [[Teams/Cadillac]] for their 2026 debut alongside [[Drivers/Sergio Pérez]].

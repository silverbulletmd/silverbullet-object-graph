Oscar Piastri won the F3 and F2 championships back to back in 2020 and 2021 as part of the [[Teams/Alpine]] junior programme, then signed with [[Teams/McLaren]] for 2023 in a celebrated contract dispute that the Contract Recognition Board resolved in his favour.

His dynamic at McLaren is one of the closest-matched in the field. Piastri took his maiden win at the 2024 Hungarian Grand Prix.

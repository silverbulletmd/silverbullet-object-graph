Charles Leclerc joined [[Teams/Ferrari]] in 2019 and has been the lead Ferrari driver through several lineup changes, partnering [[Drivers/Carlos Sainz]] and now [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] from 2025.

His Monaco home race breakthrough finally came in 2024, ending a long Monégasque drought at [[Circuits/Monaco]].

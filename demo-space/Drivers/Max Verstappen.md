Max Verstappen became the youngest Grand Prix winner in history at Spain 2016, his first race for [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]] after promotion from the [[Teams/Racing Bulls]] sister outfit. He has won four consecutive drivers' championships from 2021.

His 2026 partnership with [[Drivers/Isack Hadjar]] follows two seasons alongside [[Drivers/Sergio Pérez]]. The 2021 title fight against [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] remains one of the sport's most contentious.

Hamilton debuted with [[Teams/McLaren]] in 2007, where his relationship became one of the sport's most-discussed intra-team rivalries.

He moved to [[Teams/Mercedes]] in 2013 and won six of his seven titles there. The 2021 title against [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]] was the one that got away.

In 2025 he joined [[Teams/Ferrari]] alongside. His first Ferrari season was difficult; he stays for 2026.

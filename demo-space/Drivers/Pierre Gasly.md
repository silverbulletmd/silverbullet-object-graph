Pierre Gasly came up through the Faenza sister team and was promoted to [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]] for 2019, partnering [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]] — a difficult half-season that ended with a mid-year swap back to the sister team.

His sole win came at the chaotic 2020 Italian Grand Prix at [[Circuits/Monza]]. He moved to [[Teams/Alpine]] for 2023; his 2026 partnership with [[Drivers/Franco Colapinto]] begins this year.

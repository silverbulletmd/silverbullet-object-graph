Son of the rally champion, Carlos Sainz raced for [[Teams/McLaren]] before joining [[Teams/Ferrari]] in 2021, where his partnership lasted four seasons.

For 2025 he moved to [[Teams/Williams]] to partner [[Drivers/Alex Albon]], a long-term project under [[People/James Vowles]].

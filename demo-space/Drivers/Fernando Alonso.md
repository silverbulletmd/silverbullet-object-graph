Fernando Alonso won his two championships in 2005 and 2006. His 2007 [[Teams/McLaren]] season alongside [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] ended in acrimony.

Subsequent stints at [[Teams/Ferrari]] (2010–2014) came painfully close to a third title. After a sabbatical he returned in 2021 with [[Teams/Alpine]] and moved to [[Teams/Aston Martin]] in 2023, where his partnership continues for 2026.

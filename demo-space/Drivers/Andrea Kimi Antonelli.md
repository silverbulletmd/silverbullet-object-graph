Andrea Kimi Antonelli is the Italian phenom who skipped F2 to debut directly with [[Teams/Mercedes]] in 2025, replacing [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]]. The Mercedes junior was nurtured by [[People/Toto Wolff]] personally; his partnership continues for 2026.

His given name nods to Kimi Räikkönen, his father Marco Antonelli's racing contemporary.

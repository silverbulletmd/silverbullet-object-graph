George Russell spent three seasons learning at [[Teams/Williams]] before promotion to the works [[Teams/Mercedes]] team in 2022. He partnered [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] for three seasons; from 2025 his is the new generation pairing.

Russell is a director of the Grand Prix Drivers' Association and one of the grid's stronger qualifiers.

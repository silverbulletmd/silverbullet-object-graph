Sergio "Checo" Pérez took his first win at the 2020 Sakhir Grand Prix and parlayed that into a [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]] seat for 2021. He partnered [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]] for four seasons before being dropped at the end of 2024.

After a year out, Pérez returns in 2026 with the new [[Teams/Cadillac]] team alongside [[Drivers/Valtteri Bottas]].

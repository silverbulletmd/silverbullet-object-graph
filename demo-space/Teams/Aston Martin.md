Aston Martin returned to Formula 1 in 2021 under Lawrence Stroll, based at a new factory adjacent to Silverstone.

[[People/Adrian Newey]] joined in 2024 and now leads both technical and team-principal duties — a coup for the project. The 2026 drivers are [[Drivers/Fernando Alonso]] and [[Drivers/Lance Stroll]].

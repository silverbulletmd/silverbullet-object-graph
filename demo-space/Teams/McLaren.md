Founded by Bruce McLaren in 1966, McLaren is the second-most-successful constructor in Formula 1 history. The team has been on a strong upswing under [[People/Andrea Stella]], winning the 2024 constructors' title.

The current lineup pairs [[Drivers/Lando Norris]] with [[Drivers/Oscar Piastri]]. [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] took his 2008 title here before moving to [[Teams/Mercedes]].

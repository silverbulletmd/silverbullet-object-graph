Founded by industrialist Gene Haas, the team entered Formula 1 in 2016 as the first American constructor in three decades. Haas has a uniquely lean operating model: customer Ferrari powertrains, Dallara-built chassis, and a small UK technical base alongside the US headquarters.

[[People/Ayao Komatsu]] became team principal in 2024 after the departure of Guenther Steiner. The 2026 lineup is [[Drivers/Esteban Ocon]] (from [[Teams/Alpine]]) and [[Drivers/Oliver Bearman]], a [[Teams/Ferrari]] junior.

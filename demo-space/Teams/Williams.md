Williams Racing was founded in 1977 by Frank Williams and Patrick Head. The team won nine constructors' titles in its heyday from the 1980s through the 1990s. [[People/Adrian Newey]] designed many of those championship cars before leaving for [[Teams/McLaren]].

Under [[People/James Vowles]], who arrived from [[Teams/Mercedes]] in 2023, the team is rebuilding. [[Drivers/Carlos Sainz]] joined for 2025 from [[Teams/Ferrari]]; he partners [[Drivers/Alex Albon]].

A summary of the recent turnaround year is available in [Annual review](Resources/williams-2025-review.pdf).

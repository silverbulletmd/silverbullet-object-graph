Alpine is the current identity of the Enstone-based works team. The factory has been a championship-winning home for [[Drivers/Fernando Alonso]] in the past.

[[People/Flavio Briatore]] returned as executive advisor in 2024; [[People/Steve Nielsen]] is the managing director. The 2026 lineup is [[Drivers/Pierre Gasly]] alongside [[Drivers/Franco Colapinto]].

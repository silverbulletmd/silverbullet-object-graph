The current Mercedes works team has raced as a Mercedes works entry since 2010, based in Brackley.

[[People/Toto Wolff]] runs the team and holds an equity stake. Technical direction is handled by [[People/James Allison]]. The hybrid era from 2014 to 2021 saw [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]] and the team win eight consecutive constructors' titles together; he departed for [[Teams/Ferrari]] in 2025 and was replaced by [[Drivers/Andrea Kimi Antonelli]].

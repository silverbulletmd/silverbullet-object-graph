Audi entered Formula 1 in 2026 by acquiring the Hinwil-based Sauber operation. The full Audi factory team uses its own power unit — the first all-new German manufacturer engine in over a decade.

[[People/Mattia Binotto]], formerly of [[Teams/Ferrari]], leads the project as COO/CTO. [[People/Jonathan Wheatley]], who came across from [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]], is the team principal. The drivers are [[Drivers/Nico Hülkenberg]] and rookie [[Drivers/Gabriel Bortoleto]].

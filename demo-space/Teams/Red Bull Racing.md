Red Bull Racing entered Formula 1 in 2005. The team dominated the constructors' standings from 2010 to 2013 and returned to the top with [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]] from 2021.

For 2026 the team is led by [[People/Laurent Mekies]] after [[People/Christian Horner]] was dismissed in mid-2025. Long-time chief designer [[People/Adrian Newey]] departed for [[Teams/Aston Martin]] in 2024. Technical leadership now sits with [[People/Pierre Wache]].

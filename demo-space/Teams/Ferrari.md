Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest and most successful team in Formula 1, racing continuously since the world championship began in 1950. Based in Maranello, the team has been led by [[People/Frédéric Vasseur]] since 2023.

The 2026 lineup pairs [[Drivers/Charles Leclerc]] with [[Drivers/Lewis Hamilton]], who arrived from [[Teams/Mercedes]] in 2025.

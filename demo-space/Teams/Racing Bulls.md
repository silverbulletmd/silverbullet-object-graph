Racing Bulls is the Red Bull sister team based at the Faenza factory in Italy.

[[People/Alan Permane]] took over as principal in 2025. The team serves as the development environment for Red Bull's junior drivers — [[Drivers/Max Verstappen]] and [[Drivers/Pierre Gasly]] both came up through this route. The 2026 drivers are [[Drivers/Liam Lawson]] and rookie [[Drivers/Arvid Lindblad]].

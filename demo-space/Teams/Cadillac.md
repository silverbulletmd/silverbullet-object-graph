Cadillac F1 is the eleventh entry on the 2026 grid — the first all-new American constructor since Haas in 2016. Backed by General Motors and TWG Motorsports, the team operates from a new Indiana facility and a UK technical base. They will run customer [[Teams/Ferrari]] engines for 2026 with a GM-built power unit planned for 2028.

[[People/Graeme Lowdon]] leads the team as principal. The 2026 driver lineup pairs experienced campaigners: [[Drivers/Sergio Pérez]], returning from a year out of [[Teams/Red Bull Racing]], with [[Drivers/Valtteri Bottas]], formerly of [[Teams/Mercedes]] and Sauber.
